8Domains Sign in

Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

8Domains is a free study app run by one person. It collects as little as it can: an email address to sign you in, and your study progress so it follows you across devices. Nothing is used for analytics or advertising.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Valerio Fenni. Write to security@valerio.fyi for anything about your data.

What is collected

  • Your account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the basic profile Google shares (name and picture). Used only to sign you in.
  • Sign-in security data: IP address, browser and device of each session, kept by the sign-in provider to detect abuse and to show your active sessions.
  • Study progress: flashcard reviews and schedule, quiz answers, which concepts you opened and read, exam date and display settings. Stored in your browser and synced to your account so every device you sign in to has the same progress. Used only to show it back to you.
  • Request logs: IP address, page requested and browser, kept for a few days by the hosting provider for security and debugging.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting. The only cookies are the session cookies that keep you signed in; they are strictly necessary, so no consent banner is needed.

Who processes it

  • Clerk, Inc. handles sign-in and stores your account (privacy policy).
  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site, serves every request and stores the synced study progress in a Western European data centre (privacy policy).

Both are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on standard contractual clauses.

Why, and for how long

Account data and study progress are processed to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and kept until you delete your account; study progress is also deleted from every device and from the server when you use Me › Reset progress. Security data and logs are processed under legitimate interest in keeping the service safe (art. 6(1)(f)) and kept only as long as the providers need them for that.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing. To delete everything at once, open the app, go to Me › Manage account and delete your account. For anything else, write to the address above. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
